About EasyPay
EP
EasyPay Enterprise
Version 3.4.1 (Stable Release 2026)
ISO 27001 Certified DICGC Insured

EasyPay is India's premier digital financial ecosystem offering seamless P2P UPI transfers, high-yield digital fixed deposits, verified brand gift vouchers (Woohoo / Hubble), and direct e-commerce shopping with instant wallet settlement.

All consumer balances are protected under RBI PPI guidelines and insured up to ₹5,00,000 per user by DICGC participating partner rails.

Terms & Conditions

1. Account Registration & KYC

By creating an account, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age. All users must maintain valid KYC information. Accounts remain in 'Not Active' status until the first successful wallet deposit is completed.

2. Wallet Balances & Deposits

Deposits via UPI, Bank Transfer, or USDT are subject to admin UTR confirmation. Approved funds are instantly credited to the user's primary wallet.

3. E-Commerce Purchases & Vouchers

Products purchased on EasyPay Mall include standard brand warranty. Digital gift vouchers are delivered instantly upon purchase.

Privacy & Data Security Policy

1. Bank-Grade Encryption

All monetary transactions and credentials are encrypted using AES-256 and TLS 1.3 standards. Security PINs are hashed and never stored in plain text.

2. Zero Data Selling

We strictly adhere to RBI digital payment directives and never monetize, sell, or disclose personal customer data to uncertified third parties.
